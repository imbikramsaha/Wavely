Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Wavely does one job: play the music on your device, beautifully and reliably.

Truly offline

Plays the audio files on your phone. No sign-in, no streaming, no network required.

Background playback

Lock-screen and notification controls, headset buttons, and audio focus handled properly.

A queue that behaves

Play next, add to queue, drag to reorder, swipe to remove with undo. Shuffle and repeat too.

Playlists & favorites

Create, rename, and reorder playlists. Heart a song to keep it one tap away.

Sleep timer & speed

Drift off with a sleep timer, or change playback speed for podcasts and lectures.

Search & sort

Find any song by title or artist. Sort by title, date added, or duration.

Light, dark & Material You

Follows your system theme, with optional dynamic color from your wallpaper.

Picks up where you left off

Your queue and position are saved, so the next launch resumes right where you stopped.

Screens

Quiet by design.

Deep neutrals, one gentle gradient, and artwork that fills itself in when your files don't have any.

Privacy

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Wavely has no analytics and no servers to talk to. It doesn't request the internet permission at all, so your library and listening habits never leave your phone.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Wavely is distributed as an APK on GitHub. Requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer.

Download the APK

On your Android phone, tap Download APK. The file is about 2.7 MB.

Allow the install

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, let your browser or file manager install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Open Wavely

Grant access to Music and audio and your library appears. That's it.

Updating from an older version? Install the new APK over the existing app. Every release is signed with the same key, so your playlists and favorites stay put.
